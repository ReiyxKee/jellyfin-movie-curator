⚙ Naming & options

Tokens: {title}, {originalTitle} (falls back to title), {year}, {tmdb}, {imdb}, {tvdb}. Series also: {season}, {seasonTitle}, {episode}, {episodes} (E05 / E05-E06), {episodeTitle}, {series}. {season:00} pads numbers. Empty brackets and dangling dashes vanish when a value is missing; : becomes ：.
